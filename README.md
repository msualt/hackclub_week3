This is the render for my Stylophone. I've always thought they were cool, but also if you wanted to play it with your friends you were stuck to one instrument. I also noticed that they were just really bulky and large, as well as inconvenient to really carry. I designed this to be as thin and small as possible, and maybe to be able to carry it on a keychain if you really waned.
